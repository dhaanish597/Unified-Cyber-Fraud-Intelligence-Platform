# Fusion Commercial Model

## Consumer

Fusion SafeCheck provides free QR intelligence and explainable observed-risk guidance. It does not execute, authorize, or settle payments.

## Business

Fusion Risk API provides deterministic QR and payment-identifier assessments, sanitized signals, and usage-based or enterprise licensing.

## Enterprise

Fusion AI SOC provides tenant-scoped Operations Center workflows, session/device telemetry, investigation tooling, and annual platform licensing.

## SDK

Fusion SDK provides bank/fintech integration for authenticated device/session telemetry and risk signals.

## Compliance boundary

The current repository does not claim regulatory certification. Future compliance work should separately assess privacy notices, data retention, consent, moderation, security controls, incident response, and jurisdiction-specific payment/financial regulations.
